7 Days in Himachal: A First-Timer's Guide to the Mountains

F
Fatima Noor · Aug 3, 2026 · 8 min read

The first time you see the Himalayas from the road into Manali, it's hard not to pull over and just stare. We spent seven days making our way from the Kullu valley up into Solang and Kasol, and this is the itinerary — and the mistakes — we'd pass on to a first-timer.

Day 1–2: Settling into Manali

Give yourself a day to acclimatize before doing anything strenuous. Old Manali's cafés and the walk along the Beas river are the perfect low-key start, and the altitude here is gentle enough that most travellers won't feel it.

"The mountains don't care how fit you are on paper — they care whether you gave your body a day to catch up."

Day 3–4: Solang Valley & Snow Activities

This is where most people's Instagram feed comes from, and for good reason. Cable cars, snow tubing, and — depending on the season — paragliding with the whole valley spread out below you. Book activities a day ahead in peak season; slots fill up fast.

Day 5–7: Kasol & the Parvati Valley

The drive from Manali to Kasol takes you through some of the most dramatic scenery of the whole trip. Kasol itself is small, walkable, and full of riverside cafés — a good place to slow down after a few high-energy days in Solang.

By day seven, most first-timers leave with the same feeling: it wasn't enough time. That's honestly the right takeaway — treat this as a taste, not the whole meal.
