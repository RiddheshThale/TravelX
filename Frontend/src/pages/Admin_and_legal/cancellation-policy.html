Cancellation Policy

Clear rules for cancellations, modifications, and refunds so you can travel with peace of mind.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Overview

Voyageora acts as an intermediary between you and travel suppliers (airlines, hotels, tour operators). Cancellation and refund rules are primarily governed by the supplier’s policy, which is displayed at the time of booking. This page summarizes our general approach and service fees.

2. How to Cancel or Modify

You can request a cancellation or modification in any of the following ways:

  • Through your Voyageora account dashboard (recommended)
  • By emailing support@voyageora.com with your booking reference
  • By calling our 24/7 support line: +91 98765 43210

Please include your full name, booking ID, and preferred resolution.

3. Flight Cancellations

3.1 Airline Rules Apply

Most fares are non-refundable or carry airline-specific cancellation fees. Refundable tickets may still incur airline penalties plus our service fee.

3.2 Voyageora Service Fee

  • Cancellation within 24 hours of booking (if allowed by airline): ₹299 per passenger
  • Standard cancellation: ₹499–₹999 per passenger (depending on route and fare type)
  • No-show: Subject to full airline forfeiture + service fee

4. Hotel Cancellations

Hotel free-cancellation windows vary widely. Many properties allow free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before check-in. After the free window:

  • 1 night’s charge or a percentage of the stay may be deducted by the hotel
  • Voyageora service fee: ₹199–₹499 per booking

Non-refundable rates cannot be cancelled for a refund.

5. Holiday Package Cancellations

Package cancellations are calculated as a percentage of the total package price based on days remaining before departure:

  • 45+ days before departure: 10% of package cost
  • 30–44 days: 25% of package cost
  • 15–29 days: 50% of package cost
  • 7–14 days: 75% of package cost
  • Less than 7 days / no-show: 100% of package cost

Any non-refundable components (special flights, event tickets, etc.) will be deducted in full.

6. Refund Process

  • Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 business days after confirmation from the supplier.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
  • Bank processing times may add additional days.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is initiated.

7. Modifications

Date, name, or itinerary changes are subject to supplier availability and fees. Voyageora charges a modification service fee of ₹299–₹799 in addition to any supplier charges.

8. Special Circumstances

8.1 Medical Emergencies

With valid medical documentation, we will do our best to assist with exceptions, subject to supplier approval. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.

8.2 Force Majeure

In cases of natural disasters, pandemics, or government travel bans, we follow the supplier’s force-majeure policy and will work with you on rebooking or credits where available.

9. Travel Insurance

We highly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance at the time of booking. Insurance can cover cancellations due to illness, accidents, and other covered reasons that our standard policy may not.

10. Contact for Cancellations

Need help with a cancellation? Reach our dedicated team:

  • Email: support@voyageora.com
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +91 98765 43210 (24/7)
  • Live Chat: Available on the website and app
