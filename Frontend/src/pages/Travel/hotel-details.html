Azure Cliff Resort

5★ · Uluwatu, Bali · Ocean-view villas & infinity pool

About the hotel

★★★★★ · 4.9 (1,240 reviews)

Perched on the limestone cliffs of Uluwatu, Azure Cliff Resort offers sweeping Indian Ocean views, an infinity pool that seems to melt into the horizon, and private villas with plunge pools.

Expect refined Balinese design, a full spa, two restaurants (including cliffside fine dining), and easy access to surf breaks and Uluwatu Temple. Ideal for honeymoons and special occasions.
