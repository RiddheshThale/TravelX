Bali Bliss Escape

7 nights · Honeymoon · Flights + hotels + experiences

Package overview

★★★★★ · Ideal for couples

A carefully paced week across Ubud and Seminyak. Start with jungle calm and culture, then unwind by the beach with a private villa night, spa day and a full-day Nusa Penida excursion.

Round-trip flights from major Indian cities, 4-star stays, daily breakfast, private airport transfers and selected tours are included. Upgrade options available for rooms and experiences.

ITINERARY

  • Day 1–3 · Ubud: Arrival transfer, rice terrace walk, Monkey Forest, optional spa
  • Day 4: Transfer to Seminyak, check-in beach hotel
  • Day 5: Full-day Nusa Penida (snorkel + viewpoints)
  • Day 6: Free day / optional Uluwatu sunset & dinner
  • Day 7–8: Leisure, departure transfer

INCLUSIONS

  • Return economy flights
  • 6 nights 4★ hotels
  • 1 night private villa
  • Daily breakfast
  • Airport transfers
  • Nusa Penida tour
