Bali, Indonesia

Island of the Gods — temples, beaches, rice terraces and wellness.

About Bali

★★★★☆ · 4.7 (2,840 reviews)

Bali is Indonesia’s most famous island, known for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches and coral reefs. Hindu culture shapes daily life — from ornate temples to colourful ceremonies.

Visit Ubud for art and jungle, Canggu for surf and cafés, Uluwatu for cliffside sunsets, and Nusa islands for quieter shores. Wellness retreats, diving and volcano hikes make Bali a year-round favourite.

  • Best time: Apr – Oct
  • Language: Indonesian, English
  • Currency: Indonesian Rupiah
  • Visa: VOA available
  • Avg. stay: 5–10 days
  • Timezone: WITA (UTC+8)

TOP EXPERIENCES

  • Sunrise trek on Mount Batur
  • Ubud Monkey Forest & rice terrace walks
  • Uluwatu temple & Kecak fire dance
  • Snorkelling at Nusa Penida
  • Traditional Balinese spa & yoga
