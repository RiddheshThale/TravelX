Quick Travel Tips

Fast answers to the questions we hear most before a trip.

For domestic trips, 2–4 weeks ahead usually gets you the best rates. For peak-season international trips, aim for 2–3 months.
Layered thermals, a waterproof outer shell, insulated boots, gloves, and sunglasses — even snow days are bright. Full checklist in our Packing Tips guide above.
Carry some local cash for smaller towns and markets, but most hotels, restaurants, and larger vendors accept cards or UPI.
