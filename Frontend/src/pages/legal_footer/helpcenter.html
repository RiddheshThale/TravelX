Frequently Asked Questions

Bookings & Travel

How do I view or manage my bookings?

Go to My Bookings from your profile menu. You'll see every upcoming and past booking, along with options to view details, download vouchers, or request changes.

Can I change my travel dates after booking?

Most hotel and package bookings can be rescheduled up to 48 hours before check-in, subject to availability and any fare difference. Flight date changes depend on the airline's fare rules. Head to My Bookings and select "Modify" on the relevant booking.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms vary by booking type and how close to departure you cancel. Full details are on our Cancellation Policy page.

Payments & Refunds

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, and popular wallets. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways — we never store your full card number.

How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days, though it can take a few extra days to reflect depending on your bank or card issuer.

Account & Access

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Go to the Sign In page and click "Forgot password?" to receive a reset link by email.

How do I update my profile or notification settings?

Visit your Profile to update personal details, or Settings to manage notifications, password, and privacy preferences.

Still need help?

Our support team typically responds within a few hours.
