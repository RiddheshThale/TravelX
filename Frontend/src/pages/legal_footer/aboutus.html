About TravelX

We're a small team obsessed with making travel planning feel less like admin and more like the trip itself.

Est. 2018 · Mumbai, India

Our Story

TravelX started in 2018 with a simple frustration: booking a trip meant juggling a dozen tabs, comparing prices across sites that never agreed with each other, and hoping the hotel photos were honest. We built TravelX to put flights, hotels, and curated packages in one place — priced fairly, described accurately, and bookable in a few clicks.

What began as a two-person side project comparing flight fares for friends has grown into a full travel platform serving thousands of travellers across India and beyond, without losing the part we started with: genuinely caring whether your trip goes well.

What We Do

  • Curated domestic and international tour packages
  • Hotel and resort bookings with verified reviews
  • Flight search and booking across major airlines
  • 24/7 travel support before, during, and after your trip
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden fees at checkout

Our Mission

To make world-class travel planning accessible — combining local expertise with a platform that's honest about what you're paying for and what you're getting. We believe a good trip starts with trusting the people who planned it with you.

Our Values

Transparency First

The price you see is the price you pay. No surprise fees, no bait-and-switch listings.

Real Support, Real People

Every booking is backed by a support team that actually picks up the phone — not just a chatbot loop.

Sustainable Travel

We partner with hotels and operators who invest in their local communities and environments, and we highlight those choices so you can make them too.

By The Numbers

  • 50,000+ travellers served since 2018
  • 1,200+ hotels and resorts on the platform
  • 85+ countries covered for flights and packages
  • 4.7/5 average customer rating
